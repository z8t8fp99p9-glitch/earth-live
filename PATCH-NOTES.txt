EARTH // LIVE v1.5.38 — Cumulative Full-Screen Effect Personalisation

This single ZIP includes the recent full-screen effect timing, light, and personalisation changes. Upload index.html, style.css, and app.js to the repository root; leave all image assets and other files untouched.

Full-screen durations: Basic Earth 1.5s; HD Earth 1.5s; Live Location 2s; Sun lava 5s; Moon snow 5s; Stacks electric 1s.
Sun and Moon atmospheric light fades after 2.5s while lava/snow continues until 5s. Effect intensity and atmospheric-light on/off/intensity are separately adjustable for all six full-screen orb effects. Settings persist on the device.

Added “Restore Our Defaults” to return all six full-screen effects to EARTH // LIVE defaults: effects on, effect intensity 100%, atmospheric light on, light intensity 50%. Small-orb artwork, glow rings, shadows, and signature small-orb effects are not controlled or changed by these settings.


v1.5.39 — Fix independent light intensity
- Removed root-level atmospheric gradients that bypassed the light-intensity slider.
- Light intensity now controls the full-screen atmospheric light layers independently.
- Lava/snow particle intensity remains controlled by the separate effect-intensity slider.
- Small-orb visuals and behavior are unchanged.


v1.5.40 — Simplified effect personalisation
- Each orb now labels its first control with the actual full-screen effect (Cosmic expansion, Scanning pulse, Lava, Snow, or Electric energy) and its intensity.
- The second control remains Atmospheric light with its own toggle and intensity.
- Kept small-orb visuals outside these settings.
- Added explicit zero-intensity suppression for the atmospheric light and particle canvas.


v1.5.41 — Smooth atmospheric-light intensity
- Corrected light slider mapping to a true 0–100% multiplier.
- Removed brittle CSS text matching that could behave like an intensity threshold.
- At 0%, atmospheric light is explicitly hidden; intermediate values scale progressively.
- Effect intensity remains independent. Small-orb visuals are unchanged.


v1.5.42 — Restore visible Earth cosmic expansion
- Found that the v1.5.40 transparency rule was also clearing the cosmic effect background.
- Restricted transparent backgrounds to the particle-led Sun/Moon effects and restored the cosmic radial expansion background.
- Preserved the separate effect and light intensity controls.
