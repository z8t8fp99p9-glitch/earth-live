EARTH // LIVE v1.5.38 — Cumulative Full-Screen Effect Personalisation

This single ZIP includes the recent full-screen effect timing, light, and personalisation changes. Upload index.html, style.css, and app.js to the repository root; leave all image assets and other files untouched.

Full-screen durations: Basic Earth 1.5s; HD Earth 1.5s; Live Location 2s; Sun lava 5s; Moon snow 5s; Stacks electric 1s.
Sun and Moon atmospheric light fades after 2.5s while lava/snow continues until 5s. Effect intensity and atmospheric-light on/off/intensity are separately adjustable for all six full-screen orb effects. Settings persist on the device.

Added “Restore Our Defaults” to return all six full-screen effects to EARTH // LIVE defaults: effects on, effect intensity 100%, atmospheric light on, light intensity 50%. Small-orb artwork, glow rings, shadows, and signature small-orb effects are not controlled or changed by these settings.
