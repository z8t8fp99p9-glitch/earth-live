EARTH // LIVE — Adaptive Globe Composition v1.4.3

Purpose
- Add a modest landscape camera-space lift so the globe sits higher in landscape without scaling the Earth or shrinking the full-screen Cesium interaction surface.
- Reverse the offset when returning to portrait only while the user has not begun interacting with the globe.
- Once the user starts dragging, pinching, or scrolling the globe, orientation changes no longer override their camera position.
- Keep the deck unchanged and independent. No orb art, button controls, Stacks menu, database, or earthquake/Sun/Moon systems are added or modified.
