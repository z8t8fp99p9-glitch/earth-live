EARTH // LIVE — Adaptive Display Zoom Controls v1.4.7

Changes
- Restores the minimum camera distance to 10 metres.
- Reduces Cesium zoomFactor to 2.5 across display profiles for a gentler zoom response.
- Disables tilt, translate/pan, and look controls so the camera interaction is focused on globe rotation or zoom.
- Keeps v1.4.6 resize/camera decoupling: resize events resize the drawing surface and do not reset camera framing.
- Preserves adaptive portrait/landscape composition targets, imagery, deck, and database files.
